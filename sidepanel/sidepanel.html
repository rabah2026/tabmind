GETTING STARTED

Welcome to TabMind 🧠

🔒

100% Private, On-Device AI

TabMind interacts with and queries your open browser tabs with zero tracking and zero cloud data leaks.

🛡️
Zero Telemetry: All AI processing runs completely on your machine. No cloud accounts, external keys, or telemetry.
📑
Smart Page Context: Chat naturally with any web page, highlight text for focused queries, or switch between open tabs.
🟣

Gemini Nano: Built into Chrome

TabMind uses Chrome's built-in on-device AI by default for fast, private inference with zero extra software needed.

⚡
One-Time Auto Download: Submitting your first question triggers Chrome to download the local model weights (~2–3 GB). Live download percentage displays in chat!
🌐
Supported Languages: Optimized for English (en), with native multilingual support for Spanish (es), Japanese (ja), French (fr), and German (de).
🦙
Multi-Engine Freedom: Prefer Ollama (Llama 3.2, Mistral, DeepSeek) or LM Studio? Easily switch in Settings anytime.
⚡

Power Shortcuts & Skills

Boost your productivity with instant commands and context switching.

⌨️

🏷️

📥

Engine Settings

🟣 Gemini Nano (Chrome Built-in AI - Default):

  • Automatic Download: When you send your first prompt, Chrome lazily downloads model weights (~2–3.5 GB) with real-time percentage progress in chat.
  • Supported Languages: English (primary optimization), with expanding native support for Spanish, Japanese, French, and German.
  • Requirements: Chrome 138+ (Canary or Dev recommended if flag is missing in Stable) & at least 22 GB free disk space on drive C:.
  • Flags in chrome://flags:
    • #prompt-api (or #prompt-api-for-gemini-nano) → Enabled
    • #optimization-guide-on-device-model → Enabled BypassPerfRequirement
  • Diagnostics: Check live model status at chrome://on-device-internals or update component in chrome://components.
Compatible with LM Studio, llama.cpp, LocalAI, vLLM.
